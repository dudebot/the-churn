02 / UNDER THE SURFACE

A mechanism.
Not a prophecy.

The intuition is powerful: when trust becomes expensive, the circle of reliable cooperation can shrink. But shared danger also creates solidarity. This model lets those forces compete.

What makes the blobs change?01

Agents have different priorities and a social network. They adjust their priorities after compatible encounters. Churn reduces the range of differences they will accept; incompatible ties can be replaced by more compatible ones. Shared fate preserves connections and expands tolerance.

Each setting reruns the same starting population for a fixed number of interactions. The slider compares conditions; it does not rewind a historical timeline.

What is measured—and what is drawn?02

A cooperation group is a connected component of the remaining social network, including singletons. Connections are transitive: two members need not cooperate directly. At this finite horizon some incompatible ties can remain. Group count is not an estimate of real tribes, allegiance, hostility, or willingness to use violence.

The map fixes a two dimensional PCA projection of the starting synthetic priorities. It is not a PCA of real geopolitical data. Positions locate starting profiles, not current opinions. This reference layout keeps the same people in the same places across scenarios. Hidden dimensions still affect cooperation.

Colored fields use a fixed Gaussian kernel. Their shapes come from agent positions and group membership; the blur is not turned down by the churn slider. Colors identify groups within a scenario; the same color across scenarios does not prove group continuity. Mixed colors show projected proximity, not measured overlapping identity.

Assumptions, limits & reproducibility04

This is an uncalibrated synthesis of published mechanisms, not a replication of one paper or a validated model of societal collapse. The mapping from churn to tolerance and rewiring is an explicit scenario assumption. No study establishes a universal scale from prosperous billions to survival pairs.

There are no real people, nations, parties, famine estimates, or population multipliers behind the dots. Mutual aid is a modeled counterforce, not a fitted intervention effect. Network connectedness does not measure emotional belonging or loneliness.

Export the seed, parameters, agents, edges and metrics to reproduce a run. The repository includes equations and a multi-seed sensitivity audit. Finite-time results can differ from eventual equilibrium.
